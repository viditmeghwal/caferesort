How we work.

Four steps. Forty-eight hours. One zine. One Loom. One clarity.

Step 1

You submit

Fill out our form. Tell us your handle, main goal (followers, engagement, bookings), menu photos if you have them. That's it.

Step 2

We audit

Two humans. Real eyes on your grid. We score everything. Reels, captions, colours, hashtags, bio, voice. Live feedback on what's working.

Step 3

You get zine + Loom

14-page PDF zine with scores, heatmaps, before/afters, and your 90-day roadmap. Plus a 20-minute Loom walkthrough—us explaining it live.

Step 4

You execute

Follow the roadmap. Repost your captions. Test new reel hooks. Watch your engagement climb in 30, 60, 90 days.
