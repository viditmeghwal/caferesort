The breakdown

What's working. What's not.

✓ Grid is cohesive

Colour palette consistent. Warm earth tones (creams, mochas, burnt orange). Visual flow is smooth. Captions are long-form and thoughtful. This builds trust.

✗ Reels have weak hooks

First 0.5 seconds are slow. Viewers scroll before the payoff. You need a punch at frame 1. Try: close-ups of the espresso pour, hands-on shots, or a quick question to stop scroll.

✓ Captions tell a story

You're not just posting coffee—you're sharing the ritual, the sourcing, the relationship with farmers. This is authentic. Keep it.

✗ Bio doesn't drive action

You link to a Google Maps. Visitors don't know what to expect. Rewrite the bio to lead with "Specialty espresso + pastries in Panjim" and a link to your menu or booking page.
