Chapter Two — The Method

Slow audits. Hot takes. $15.

Most audits are a Loom recorded in one hungover afternoon. Ours takes two mornings, two humans, and lands in your inbox as a printable zine you'd actually want on your shelf.

The ritual

Four steps. Zero fluff.

01/04
Step 01

You fill a sixty-second form.

Handle, one-sentence goal, a menu upload if you've got one, fifteen dollars. That's it. No discovery calls, no NDAs, no "let me get on your calendar." You go make coffee for a customer; we start reading your grid.

— Deliver: instant receipt · brief confirmation
Step 02

We spend two mornings with your feed.

Two senior strategists. Ninety days of your content, three competitor accounts of our choosing, your hashtag spread, your bio, and — if you use them — your DM autoresponders. Roughly six to eight human hours per audit. No AI writing the report.

— Deliver: internal working doc · scoring matrix
Step 03

You receive a tight, printable zine.

A 14-page PDF laid out like an editorial magazine — you can print it, pin it, hand it to your manager. Bundled with a 20-minute Loom where we walk you through it in plain language. Delivered within 48 hours of booking.

— Deliver: PDF zine · 20-min Loom
Step 04

You ship. We stay on call.

Two weeks of async email follow-up included. Reply with reels-in-progress, caption drafts, bio edits — we'll respond in a day. No retainer. No "would you like to hop on a strategy call?" We built this so we never have to say those words.

— Deliver: 14-day inbox access · no upsell
