The verdict, in short

Beautiful feed. A little too polite.

You have a real aesthetic and real product — the flat whites look like flat whites, the light is honest, the grid has a colour story. What you don't have is a voice, a hook, or a reason for anyone in Assagao to pick you over the six other cafés within a five-minute scooter ride.

Reels are cinematic when they should be conversational. Captions describe the drink instead of the moment. And your bio is doing exactly the wrong job — telling us the address instead of telling us why we should care.

Section 05 — 90-day roadmap

Six moves, in priority order.

01

Launch a Barista Explains reel series — 2× weekly.

Same barista, same lens, one bean or drink per reel. This is the format that's already working (see Reel B). Scale it before anything else.

Week 01 Highest impact
02

Add a text hook to every reel cover.

Six words max. It should promise something specific — a taste, a lesson, a hot take. If you can't write the hook, the reel isn't ready to post.

Week 01 Quick win
03

Cut posts to three per week. Make them bigger.

You're posting five times to say the same thing. Consolidate. Three sharper, better-lit, better-written posts will out-perform five polite ones every time.

Week 02 Cadence
04

Route DMs to bookings — one automation.

ManyChat or Meta's native tool. Trigger word "table," reply with a reservation link. Estimated recovery: ~₹28,000/month. Two hours of setup.
